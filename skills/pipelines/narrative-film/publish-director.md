# Publish Director — Narrative Film Pipeline

## When to use

After the film renders and passes final review. You package the deliverable and
its derivatives. Requires human approval before publishing anywhere external.

## Output artifact

`publish_log` (schema: `schemas/artifacts/publish_log.schema.json`).

## Process

1. **Label the hero export** and any derivatives (aspect-ratio variants, social
   cutdowns, trailer) clearly.
2. **Metadata** — title, logline-derived description, and tags that match the
   film's tone and distribution intent. Pull from the `story_bible`.
3. **Poster/thumbnail** — propose a hero frame for the poster.
4. **Confirm before external publish.** Sending to any external service is
   outward-facing; get explicit user approval first.

## Review focus

- Hero export and derivatives are clearly labeled.
- Metadata matches tone and distribution intent.
- Export package contains video and metadata outputs.
