# Edit Director — Brand Narrative Pipeline

## When to use

After assets. You make the cut decisions for a tight social edit and record them
in `edit_decisions`. Auto stage.

## Output artifact

`edit_decisions` (schema: `schemas/artifacts/edit_decisions.schema.json`).

## Process

1. **Cut tight for social.** Fast pacing, motion early, no dead air. Respect the
   brief duration.
2. **Carry `render_runtime` unchanged.** Copy the locked proposal value into
   `edit_decisions` verbatim — a silent swap is a CRITICAL governance violation.
3. **Place persistent brand elements** — logo overlay and end-card CTA — as edit
   decisions, honoring the brand safe-area.
4. Time music (brand `music_style`) and any sonic logo to the cut.

## Review focus

- Pacing is tight for social.
- `render_runtime` carried unchanged.
- Logo and CTA are placed within the safe-area.
